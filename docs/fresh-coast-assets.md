# Fresh Coast carousel assets

Local implementation of concept 01, pending user review. Do not deploy without explicit approval.
The original logo file is unchanged. Custom carousel uploads from the administration panel remain supported; only the original bundled demo images resolve to the refreshed defaults.

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool, using the approved concept 01 board as a photographic style reference. No CLI/API fallback was used. Original generated PNGs were copied without editing.

## Saved assets and prompts

Shared prompt: Create one continuous full-bleed professional seafood photograph for a website carousel, not a board or UI. Natural cool daylight, true-to-life texture, vivid but restrained ocean-blue/turquoise mood, natural silver and orange fish colors. Landscape 3:2 composition, main food grouped in central 65% with breathing room for responsive crops. Crushed ice glints, premium appetizing editorial style. No text, logos, graphic waves, arrows, buttons, dots, watermarks, frames or shellfish.

- `public/assets/hero-fresh-v2.png`: Two anatomically realistic whole fresh silver sea bream diagonally arranged on crushed clear ice, one luminous orange salmon fillet at lower right, a halved yellow lemon, small fresh thyme sprigs, subtle folded navy-striped linen at an edge. Fresh abundant fish-market still life like concept 01.
- `public/assets/hero-salmon-v2.png`: A pristine luminous orange fresh salmon fillet with natural fine white fat lines diagonally arranged on crushed clear ice on a dark navy ceramic platter, lemon wedge and fresh thyme. Salmon is the main subject.
- `public/assets/hero-fillets-v2.png`: Two to three pristine raw white sea bass fillets with delicate silver skin along an edge on crushed ice and a deep navy surface, a lemon wedge and subtle fresh herbs. Natural soft white flesh.

Desktop and mobile compositions are handled by responsive CSS, with live HTML headings and controls. The first frame has no baked-in UI. There is no automatic rotation; customers advance with buttons or keyboard arrows.
